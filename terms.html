Available help may include towing and flatbed transport, two-wheeler recovery, accident recovery, battery assistance, tyre help, fuel delivery, minor roadside repairs and driver services. Availability depends on your location, vehicle, road access, crew and equipment.

  • Do not treat an app, website or phone response-time estimate as a guarantee that a vehicle has been dispatched. Wait for confirmation from our team.

3. Prices, estimates and payment

Published “from” prices and membership benefits are starting points, not a final quote for every job. The final price can depend on the vehicle, distance, route, waiting time, time of day, recovery complexity, equipment, tolls and other disclosed third-party costs.

We will tell you the applicable charges and any material change in scope before proceeding with chargeable additional work. Ask the dispatcher to confirm the total or calculation before authorising dispatch. If the job changes after arrival, the crew should explain the change and obtain your approval before continuing where practicable.

Payment methods, applicable taxes and any advance payment will be shown or explained when you book. Pay only through the payment method confirmed by RACE or its authorised payment provider, and retain the receipt or transaction reference. Do not pay an unquoted personal charge to a crew member; contact our helpline if an amount is disputed.

6. Your responsibilities and roadside safety

You must be the vehicle owner or have the owner’s permission to request towing or repairs. You are responsible for accurate information, access to the vehicle, lawful instructions and any documents reasonably needed to establish authority.

  • Move to a safe place away from moving traffic where possible, switch on hazard lights and follow instructions from police, road authorities and our dispatcher.
  • Keep passengers, especially children, away from the carriageway and work area. Do not ask a crew to perform work in an unsafe location.
  • Remove cash, valuables, personal items and important documents before the vehicle is transported. Tell the crew about loose, fragile or special items that cannot be removed.
  • Disclose modifications, low ground clearance, all-wheel drive, electric or hybrid systems, locked wheels and any other condition that could affect loading or towing.

7. Vehicle inspection, custody and delivery

Before loading, review the vehicle condition with the crew where it is safe to do so. Take or review photographs, note visible damage, confirm the destination and keep the job receipt or condition report. At delivery, inspect the vehicle and promptly report any concern to support with the booking reference and photographs.

We will take reasonable care while the vehicle is in our or our assigned crew’s custody. Nothing in these terms excludes responsibility for loss or damage caused by our negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of an obligation that cannot lawfully be excluded. Pre-existing damage, inaccessible areas and items left inside should be documented where practical.
